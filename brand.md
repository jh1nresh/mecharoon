# Mecharoon Brand System

## Brand overview

**Product:** Mecharoon

**Category:** Agent Spend Control Plane

**Core promise:** Every autonomous transaction is delegated deliberately, reserved atomically, and reconciled with an auditable receipt.

Mecharoon should feel like financial infrastructure with judgment: exact, calm, and accountable. It is not a wallet, a payment rail, a crypto protocol, or an AI character. The identity visualizes a control system that can constrain action without slowing it down.

## Identity idea

### The authority aperture

The symbol is built from three nested incomplete rounded-bracket boundaries around one transaction point.

- The **outer band** is the principal authority boundary.
- The **middle band** is delegated policy scope.
- The **inner band** is the child agent's executable allowance.
- The **transaction point** is the budget reservation: one amount, one intent, one atomic commitment.
- Each boundary is split at its crown and remains open below. The nested negative channels create two outer legs and a central notch, suggesting an `M` without adding a separate letter.
- The downward aperture leads to the transaction point; the same controlled path returns outward as reconciliation. Execution is not complete until the reservation resolves into a receipt.

The mark is intentionally geometric, open, and procedural. It should never imply physical custody or absolute security.

### Construction

- Base grid: `64 × 64`.
- Center: `32, 32`.
- Three vertically nested rounded-bracket boundaries with visibly different scales.
- Rounded terminals only.
- The crown notches remain separated so all three boundaries are legible at `16px`.
- The transaction point sits on the vertical centerline inside the narrowest executable boundary.
- Master geometry uses strokes of `5.5`, `4.5`, and `3.5` on the `64 × 64` grid; the point is centered at `32, 47` with radius `3.4`.
- In the color mark, bands are Deep Ink and the point is Reserved Green.
- In the one-color mark, every element uses the same color.

Do not add shadows, gradients, outlines around the entire mark, decorative nodes, connecting lines, or additional rings.

## Wordmark

The wordmark is `Mecharoon`, not all caps in primary product contexts.

The double `oo` is the only customized letter pair:

- The first `o` is the **parent authority** and remains at full x-height.
- The second `o` is the **child authority** at approximately `78%` of the parent's diameter.
- Both remain optically related but physically separate; the child does not sit inside or touch the parent.
- The child is optically centered on the parent's horizontal axis, with a subtle downward offset allowed at large sizes.
- A floating Reserved Green handoff point may appear between the pair in display lockups. It must touch neither ring. Remove it in one-color and small-size applications.

Never exaggerate the pair into bubbles, eyes, chain links, infinity loops, or a mascot expression.

## Logo configurations

### Primary lockup

Symbol on the left, followed by the `Mecharoon` wordmark. Use for navigation, product chrome, sales material, and presentation covers.

### Symbol

Use alone for the favicon, app icon, avatar, compact control surfaces, and diagram nodes.

### One-color

The one-color mark is the master durability test. Use Deep Ink on light surfaces or Warm Off-White on dark surfaces. The central point must not disappear; it takes the same color as the bands.

### Minimum sizes

- Symbol: `16px` minimum digital size.
- Primary lockup: `112px` minimum digital width.
- Below `24px`, use the symbol only and use the one-color construction.
- At `16px`, preserve all three bracket boundaries, their separated crown notches, and the center point. Do not reduce stroke opacity.

### Clear space

Use one inner-band diameter as clear space around the symbol. For the primary lockup, use the height of the lowercase `o` on all sides.

| Foreground / background | Ratio | Use |
|---|---:|---|
| Deep Ink / Warm Off-White | `14.98:1` | All text sizes |
| Ink Soft / Warm Off-White | `8.51:1` | All text sizes |
| Muted / Warm Off-White | `4.87:1` | Body and metadata |

| Warm Off-White / Reserve Dark | `6.75:1` | Reverse text and buttons |

Reserved Green is a state-bearing color, not decoration. Keep it under roughly `10%` of a typical page and use it for reservation, permission, or verified reconciliation moments. Red and amber may appear only as semantic risk/status colors in product data; they are not brand colors.

Do not use gradients.

## Typography

### Interface and marketing

Use **Geist Sans** for product UI, landing pages, decks, and prose.

- Headings: `600–700`.
- Body: `400–500`.
- Labels: `600`, with restrained tracking.
- Prefer sentence case. Avoid long all-caps headlines.

### Ledger and evidence

Use **Geist Mono** for amounts, policy identifiers, timestamps, receipt hashes, rail names, and event labels. Monospace is evidence, not atmosphere; do not set paragraphs or entire pages in it.

### Wordmark

The product wordmark is a custom treatment based on Geist's proportions. Do not recreate it by typing a standard font and leaving both `o` glyphs unchanged.

## Voice

Mecharoon speaks like an accountable operator: concise, precise, and calm under pressure.

### Principles

- **State the control.** Say what was authorized, reserved, executed, or reconciled.
- **Name the boundary.** Include amount, scope, actor, rail, and time when they matter.
- **Show proof.** Prefer a receipt or event state over a superlative.
- **Separate permission from payment.** Mecharoon governs spend; it does not pretend to be every payment rail.
- **Avoid fear.** Explain risk without threat language or security theater.

### Preferred language

- “Reserve $240 for this task.”
- “Authorized within the child agent's daily limit.”
- “Reservation released; no charge settled.”
- “Reconciled to one receipt across the selected rail.”

### Avoid

- “Unbreakable security”
- “Magic AI wallet”
- “Crypto-native treasury”
- “Your agent is now unstoppable”
- “Guaranteed fraud prevention”

## Visual language

Use:

- Nested boundaries
- Ledger rows and receipts
- Deliberate spacing
- Thin structural rules
- Real transaction states
- One focused Reserved Green proof point

Avoid:

- Shields
- Locks
- Coins or currency symbols as the primary mark
- Robots, faces, or mascots
- Chain links
- Pastries or macaroons
- Nodes-and-neon crypto aesthetics
- Circuit-board textures
- Glowing gradients
- Generic AI sparkles
- Radar, broadcast, or Wi-Fi treatment

Never reconstruct the symbol as concentric circles. The nested M-aperture brackets are deliberately non-radial so the mark cannot collapse into broadcast waves or a target.

## Application rules

- Warm Off-White is the default brand canvas; Deep Ink is the default text color.
- Use the symbol at the start of a control flow, not as a repeated decorative bullet.
- Let product evidence carry the page: budget, authorization, reservation, settlement, and reconciliation receipts.
- Keep cards flat or nearly flat. Boundaries should come from line, spacing, and hierarchy, not heavy shadow.
- Motion, when present, should show delegation inward and reconciliation outward. Avoid pulsing, orbiting, or continuous scanning.
- The dark surface is Deep Ink with Warm Off-White type. Reserved Green remains a small active-state accent.
- No gradient, blur bloom, glassmorphism, or metallic rendering.

## Review checklist

- Does the symbol remain identifiable at `16px` in one color?
- Are all three bands and the center point visible?
- Are the three M-aperture boundaries distinct rather than concentric or wave-like?
- Does the double `oo` clearly show parent and child authority?
- Is Reserved Green attached to a real reservation or proof state?
- Is the product described as a control plane rather than a payment rail?
- Are prohibited security, crypto, AI-character, and pastry motifs absent?
- Do text and control colors meet the contrast receipts above?
